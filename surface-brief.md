# Spatial portfolio surface
Visitor mode: Experience, with focused asset/prompt inspection.
## Direction contract
THESIS: The portfolio itself is a navigable 3D archive. Every visit starts inside the world; no scrolling landing page surrounds it.
OWN-WORLD: Reference-pinned bryangarage.dev/showcase: warm peach fog, burnt orange terrain, cream atmospheric light, charcoal sculpted project tiles, pixel typography and precise spatial navigation. Real asset previews remain intact.
STORY: Travel through the 27 works by wheel, swipe, arrows, category and timeline. Open the selected artifact to inspect the work and a reconstructed prompt together; return to the same position in the world.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-viewport world, selected work prominent at center with neighboring artifacts receding into depth. Pixel project title at upper left; small utilities upper right; category navigation and travel timeline below; open-work action lower right. Mobile preserves the same single world with compact controls.
FORM: Directly requested evolution of the supplied reference world. Improve material, atmosphere, composition and behavior; preserve established portfolio content. The v3 atmosphere uses real depth-tested motes, buoyant panels and hover lift, drifting light and haze, travel gusts and ground ripples, and restrained camera inertia.
INTERACTION: Sound remains explicit opt-in, with layered locally synthesized hover, travel, open, close and confirmation cues; bounded overlap and media/hidden-document silence. Motion pause and reduced motion preserve a static world with instant navigation. Animate continuously only while ambient motion is active and the scene is visible; draw on demand when paused. Do not claim a benchmarked frame rate.
TOUCH: Preserve the same world on iPad, including trackpad-equipped devices identified by any-pointer:coarse. Use 44px primary controls and safe areas, stacked inspection at 768–900px and columns on wider landscape tablets, short-landscape camera pullback, owned/cancellable gestures without finger hover, and a 16px Index search field fitted to visualViewport. Bound tablet rendering to 420 motes, eight fragments, DPR 1.4, half-resolution procedural textures and 1200px artwork; phones below 650px use 230/six, DPR 1.35 and 1024px artwork. The configured touch ambient cap is 30fps, with normal interaction cadence; physical-device performance is not benchmarked.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
